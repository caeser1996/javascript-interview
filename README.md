## 🔄 Revision Strategy

### Daily Revision (30 minutes)
- Review one topic from Quick Revision folder
- Solve 2 coding problems

### Weekly Revision (2 hours)
- Go through all concepts learned that week
- Solve 10 coding problems from different patterns
- Mock interview practice

### Before Interview (1 day)
- Quick revision cheatsheets
- Review common pitfalls
- Practice top 20 coding problems
- Review MongoDB aggregation pipeline

## 📖 How to Use This Repository

1. **Start with fundamentals**: Don't skip JavaScript basics even if you're experienced
2. **Practice coding daily**: Consistency is key - solve at least 2 problems daily
3. **Use the cheatsheets**: Before interviews, review the quick revision materials
4. **Track your progress**: Check off items in the study plan as you complete them
5. **Focus on patterns**: Understanding patterns > memorizing solutions

## 💡 Interview Tips

1. **Think out loud**: Explain your thought process during interviews
2. **Ask clarifying questions**: Don't make assumptions about requirements
3. **Start simple**: Begin with a brute force solution, then optimize
4. **Test your code**: Walk through examples and edge cases
5. **Know time/space complexity**: Be ready to analyze your solutions
